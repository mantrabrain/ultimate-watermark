# Ultimate Watermark - Free vs Pro Features

## Executive Summary

Ultimate Watermark is currently a comprehensive free plugin that offers enterprise-level watermarking capabilities. Based on market analysis and competitor research, we can strategically position a Pro version that adds advanced features for professional users, agencies, and enterprise customers while maintaining the robust free version for individual users and small businesses.
